## Compensation
Read the preferred compensation range from local configuration. It is a preference, not a hard gate. Legitimate junior roles with meaningful work, mentorship, training, benefits, or certification support may remain eligible below the preference. Never inflate seniority to chase compensation.

Label compensation below $52,000 or $25/hour as a low-pay exception for user review. A legitimate junior role should not be rejected solely for falling below the preferred salary.

## Fit boundaries

Retain:

- junior, associate, entry-level, level I, technician, specialist, and early-career matches across the primary lanes;
- close matches;
- slight stretches with a one-to-two-year experience gap or a small tool gap;
- selected underqualified roles where task overlap is strong, the work accelerates learning, and there is no senior leadership requirement;
- roles that develop AI voice agents, conversational AI, speech applications, telephony integrations, backend engineering, VoIP/UC, support engineering, QA automation, networking, systems, or production troubleshooting skills.

Reject:

- manager, director, principal, architect, or lead roles requiring people leadership;
- roles requiring more than five years of directly relevant experience unless the requirement is explicitly preferred and other evidence is unusually strong;
- jobs whose core work is unrelated to the primary or secondary lanes;
- jobs requiring a completed license, clearance, degree, citizenship status, or certification the candidate has not confirmed;
- fraudulent, unsafe, unpaid, commission-only, or materially misleading jobs; low compensation alone is not an automatic rejection when the role is legitimate and career-building;
- commutes beyond 75 minutes without an explicit user-approved exception.
